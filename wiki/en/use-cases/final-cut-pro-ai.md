## Keep the editor you know

FrameKit takes a different approach from a replacement AI editor. It connects
supported AI agents to Final Cut so you can inspect context, express an editing
goal in plain language, review a proposed operation, and apply only capabilities
advertised by the active backend.

## A review-first workflow

1. Understand the current timeline context.
2. Tell the agent what you want to change.
3. Review the proposed operation and warnings.
4. Apply a supported operation in Final Cut or to a managed artifact.

The bundled live Workflow Extension currently provides metadata rather than a
canonical writable timeline. Guarded native UI operations are experimental and
require explicit opt-in. See the live capability cards before starting.
