AI can support a Final Cut workflow at several layers: understanding media,
finding relevant moments, planning edits, and automating explicitly supported
operations. The useful question is not whether an editor is “AI powered,” but
which parts of the workflow are observable, reviewable, and reversible.

## Start with a bounded task

Choose a task such as inspecting the current context or locating a selected
range. Check the active integration's capabilities, ask for a proposal, and
review its target before any mutation. Keep artifact edits and changes to the
open Final Cut timeline clearly separated.

FrameKit takes a bridge approach: it connects AI agents to the editor you
already use and fails closed when a requested capability is unavailable.
