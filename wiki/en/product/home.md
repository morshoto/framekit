FrameKit is the bridge between your AI and Final Cut Pro. It gives agents a
capability-aware way to understand editing context, propose supported actions,
and work with guarded editing surfaces while Final Cut remains your editor.

FrameKit is an open-source agentic video editing runtime built on MCP. The
technical details are there when you need them—not before you understand the
value.
